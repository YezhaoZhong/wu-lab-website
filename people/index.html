Dr. Qin Wu

wuqin@him.cas.cn

Qin Wu is a Principal Investigator and Ph.D. Supervisor at the Hangzhou Institute of Medicine, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Head of the Research Group. She serves as Chief Scientist of a CAS Strategic Priority Research Program, Deputy Director of the Center for Nucleic Acid Molecular Medicine, and Vice Dean of the Innovation Research Institute. She is a recipient of the National Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas), the Zhejiang Provincial Distinguished Young Scientists Fund, and is recognized as a Zhejiang High-Level Overseas Talent (Long-term Innovation Program).

Her research group integrates chemical biology, cell biology, molecular biology, and bioinformatics to construct single-cell spatiotemporal molecular maps of tumors. The team aims to identify novel therapeutic targets, elucidate the molecular mechanisms underlying tumor initiation and progression, discover predictive biomarkers of drug response, and develop innovative therapeutic strategies to enable personalized precision medicine. In the past two years, as corresponding author, she has published multiple papers in leading journals including Nature Chemical Biology, Nature Communications, Cell Chemical Biology, and Advanced Science, and has been granted several patents. She currently leads multiple national and provincial research projects, including grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China and key R&D programs of the Ministry of Science and Technology and Zhejiang Province.
