# Research concept illustrations

Four original AI-generated conceptual illustrations for the homepage Research cards.
Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. These illustrate research themes and do not represent experimental data, molecular structures determined in this project, or clinical validation.

- `discover.png`: aptamer recognition at the cell surface, sequencing and single-cell readouts.
- `design.png`: structure-guided complementarity between an aptamer and a target binding pocket.
- `engineer.png`: engineered aptamer interactions and regulation of cellular signaling.
- `translate.png`: biomarkers, patient stratification and individualized intervention concepts.

Shared visual brief: square scientific editorial schematics; deep navy background (#10273e); mint (#67d9cc) and pale blue (#a9cfe5); large legible elements; restrained shading; no text, numbers, plots, logos or paper panels. Detailed generation prompts are recorded in `prompts.json`.
