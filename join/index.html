The lab currently has openings for Master’s and PhD students in 2025. Please email Prof. Qin Wu at wuqin@ibmc.ac.cn for inquiries.

Research Directions

Functional Nucleic Acid Tools

Develop aptamer screening methods for undruggable proteins and protein interactions; build cell-based selection and target identification for membrane proteins; generate high-throughput aptamer libraries for cell atlas construction.

Single-Cell Omics & Multi-omics Analysis

Develop aptamer-based tools for single-cell surface proteomics, interactomics, and spatial multi-omics to systematically decode tumor evolution.

New Targets & Cell Therapy

Use single-cell and spatial multi-omics on clinical samples to identify therapeutic targets and develop CAR‑T and other cell therapies.

Desired Background

Bioinformatics, immunology, structural biology, cell biology, molecular biology, pharmacy, biomedical sciences, or related fields. Experience in omics data analysis is preferred.

Postdoctoral Recruitment Requirements

Abide by laws and regulations, strong academic record, and good physical and mental health.

Typically under 35 years old; PhD graduates or within 3 years of PhD completion.

Strong academic background, research potential, and team spirit; major aligns with lab needs; able to work full-time on postdoctoral research.

Postdoctoral Compensation & Benefits

Annual salary RMB 300,000 (pre-tax) plus performance incentives; RMB 400,000 (pre-tax) during national fellowship funding for selected programs.

Same benefits as institute staff, including cafeteria allowance (RMB 6,600/year), union benefits, and VIP physical exams at top-tier hospitals.

Five social insurances and one housing fund as required by national policy.

Outstanding postdocs will be prioritized for long-term positions, with talent recruitment benefits. The institute has a strategic partnership with Hangzhou Medical College, offering priority access to institutional teaching positions after postdoc completion.

How to Apply
