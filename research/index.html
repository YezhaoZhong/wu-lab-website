Research

Explore: Functional Nucleic Acid Intelligence

Through the deep integration of artificial intelligence (AI)-driven computer vision and high-throughput multimodal data analysis, we aim to build a next-generation platform for targeted nucleic acid delivery to cell surface molecules. By overcoming technical bottlenecks and enabling de novo design and high-throughput screening, we will generate high-performance molecular tool libraries, laying the core foundation for constructing high-resolution cellular atlases.

Decode: Frontier Omics Technologies

Focusing on major cancers such as pancreatic cancer and breast cancer, we leverage independently developed functional nucleic acid tools, including nucleic acid adaptors, and integrate them with advanced omics technologies. We develop single-cell (surface) proteomics, interactomics, and spatial multi-omics technologies to construct panoramic, high-resolution molecular maps of tumor initiation and progression, and systematically dissect the key driving mechanisms underlying tumor evolution.

Protect: Precision Diagnosis and Therapeutic Translation

Based on the precise identification of key cell surface molecules and their interactions that drive tumor progression, we develop novel membrane protein-targeted intervention strategies and innovative cell-based therapies. In collaboration with clinical partners, we establish a closed-loop system from multi-omics detection to clinical intervention, providing implementable solutions for personalized medicine and illuminating the path toward improved patient outcomes.
