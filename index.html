Wu Qin Research Laboratory

Our lab focuses on precision oncology and has established an integrated research framework of “technology development – mechanistic investigation – clinical translation.”

By developing high-performance molecular probes and integrating cutting-edge technologies such as single-cell sequencing and spatial proteomics, the group builds intelligent multi-omics data analysis models to systematically map the spatiotemporal dynamics of tumor initiation and progression.

The ultimate goal is to develop novel targeted therapeutic strategies and companion diagnostic biomarkers, thereby enabling personalized precision treatment.
